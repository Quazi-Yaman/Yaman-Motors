YAMAN MOTORS

Factory Billing & Business Management System

Project Documentation • System Flow • AWS Architecture • Interface

1. Project Summary

YAMAN MOTORS is a centralized factory billing and business management system designed to manage the major business operations of a factory through one web-based interface.

The system connects: Vendors, Customers, Products, Purchases, Sales, Invoices, Payments, Inventory and Reports.

The main objective is to reduce manual work, centralize business data, connect related transactions and provide a single platform for managing factory billing operations.

4. Application Interface

The application is divided into modules so that each business activity has its own dedicated section.

Module Purpose
Dashboard Provides an overall view of sales, purchases, invoices, payments, inventory and recent activities.
Vendors Stores and manages supplier information.
Customers Stores and manages customer information.
Products Maintains product and item master information.
Purchases Records products purchased from vendors.
Sales Records products sold to customers.
Invoices Manages billing documents and invoice information.
Payments Tracks payments made to vendors and received from customers.
Inventory Tracks stock movement and available quantity.
Reports Provides consolidated business information for management.

8. Vendor Invoice Processing Flow

A major part of the system is the vendor invoice processing workflow. A vendor invoice can be uploaded instead of manually entering every field.

1. Upload Invoice Vendor invoice PDF or image is uploaded.
↓
2. Store Invoice The document is stored in Amazon S3.
↓
3. Process Invoice AWS Lambda performs invoice-processing logic.
↓
4. Extract Information Vendor, invoice number, items, quantity, rate, tax and totals are extracted.
↓
5. Calculate The system calculates line amounts and totals.
↓
6. Verify Extracted totals are compared with calculated totals.
↓
7. Generate Receipt A standardized receipt is generated.
↓
8. Store / Deliver The result can be stored, viewed or downloaded.

12. AWS Services Used

AWS Service Purpose
Amazon CloudFront Delivers the YAMAN MOTORS web interface securely through HTTPS and CDN infrastructure.
Amazon S3 Stores frontend website files and vendor invoice documents.
Amazon RDS - SQL Server Main relational database for business information.
AWS Lambda Performs serverless invoice-processing and automation tasks.
API Gateway Provides API endpoints for applicable frontend/backend communication.
IAM Controls permissions and access between AWS resources.
CloudWatch Provides logs and monitoring for AWS components.
DynamoDB Used in the invoice-processing architecture for NoSQL invoice records where applicable.

15. End-to-End Example

Example: A vendor sends YAMAN MOTORS an invoice for purchased products.
  1. The user uploads the vendor invoice.
  2. The invoice document is stored in S3.
  3. Invoice-processing logic extracts the required information.
  4. Product quantity and rates are used to calculate amounts.
  5. Tax, discount and final total are validated.
  6. A sequential vendor receipt number is generated.
  7. Purchase and invoice information is recorded.
  8. Inventory is updated with the received quantity.
  9. Vendor payable is tracked.
  10. Payment can later be recorded against the payable.
  11. The transaction becomes available for reporting.

17. Conclusion

YAMAN MOTORS is designed as a centralized factory billing and business management system.

It connects vendors, customers, products, purchases, sales, invoices, payments, inventory and reporting through one application.

The AWS architecture provides the infrastructure required for hosting, storage, processing, database management, security and monitoring.

The key advantage of the system is that business operations are connected rather than isolated. A purchase can affect the invoice, inventory, payable and reports, while a sale can affect the invoice, inventory, receivable and reports.
